# **Helldivers 2 Full Tables: Reference**

> Complete per-item ratings for every primary, secondary, support weapon, throwable, stratagem, armor passive, and booster in Helldivers 2, plus a lookup mapping every armor passive to the specific armor sets that carry it. Nothing is clumped or summarised here. For the decision layer (what to actually bring), see `helldivers-2_tier-list.md`.

## Provenance and staleness

> [!danger] Two different patch stamps in this document
> Game version is **7.0.0 "Devoid of Liberty"**, released 12 August 2026. Only the **armor passive** table on u.gg has been restamped to 7.0.0. Every other u.gg table is still stamped **6.3.1 (July 2026)**, meaning those ratings are pre-patch community consensus. Items marked ⚠ are affected by a known 7.0.0 change that the rating has not yet absorbed.

- **R-2124 Constitution**: full stripper clip reload when the magazine is empty. Still rated D/D/D on pre-patch votes; expect movement.

> It ties Med-Kit as the only other S+ across all three fronts, and it is a straight damage-uptime passive rather than a survivability one. +30% support weapon reload is enormous on Autocannon, Recoilless and Grenade Launcher. > [!failure] Two passives the old doc rated too generously
> **Feet First** was in the "B or C" clump. It is actually B/D/C, which makes it near-useless on bugs. **Servo-Assisted** is C/B/C, not B. Neither is worth a slot outside a very specific stealth or throw-range build.

### Armor weight

Relative to Medium:

- **Heavy**: 10% slower, 25% damage reduction.
- **Light**: 10% faster, 25% additional damage taken.

Guidance: Light against Terminids and Illuminate Mindless Masses so you can outrun melee swarms. Heavy against Terminid Predator and Rupture strains, since you will get hit regardless of speed. Medium or Heavy against Automatons, because bot fire is ranged and hard to dodge in the open.

Helmets and capes have **no mechanical effect whatsoever**. Helmets display 100 across armor, speed and stamina but do nothing. The only exception is indirect: if the body armor's rating exceeds 100, headshot damage is reduced.

## 7.0.0 changes not covered elsewhere

- **New Illuminate enemies.** The **Crusher** has an enormous health pool and regenerates while taking heavy damage, so it must be worn down continuously without pause. The **Wretch** dodges and leaps while closing, and attempts to disarm you. Both favour sustained DPS over burst, which is an argument for Stalwart, Bullet Storm, Flamethrower and Cremator over one-shot anti-tank on squid fronts.
- **SEAF support** is now a planet modifier. Friendly SEAF NPCs spawn with stim pistols and heavy machine guns, and can destroy enemy spawners.
- **Level cap raised** from 150 to 300, with 15 new ranks.
- **Emote wheel** expanded from 4 to 8 slots.
- **Heavy landing** animation cut from 1.32s to 0.8s, roughly a 40% reduction.
- **Stim input buffering**: a stim press is remembered for up to 2 seconds and fires as soon as the action becomes legal.
- **GATER** received a Super V48 Interceptor engine, plus increased ammo capacity, health, knockback and impact damage. Enemies no longer spontaneously attack it unless a Helldiver is inside or it is drilling.
- **Loadout icons** now show a backpack icon on stratagem weapons requiring a backpack, and a drop-weapon icon on those that cannot be reloaded.
- **Hit markers** no longer display for indirect explosive damage, your own sentries, or your own orbitals and airstrikes. Melee, laser and flame weapons now display correctly.
