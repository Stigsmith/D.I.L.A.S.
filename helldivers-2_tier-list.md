# **Helldivers 2 Tier List: Reference**

> The decision layer. What to actually bring, per faction, at Difficulty 7 and above. Current as of Patch 7.0.0 "Devoid of Liberty", 12 August 2026. For every item and every rating without clumping, plus the full armor passive lookup, see `helldivers-2_tables.md`.

## Read this first

> [!danger] Most ratings here predate the current patch
> Patch 7.0.0 landed on 12 August 2026. u.gg has restamped only its **armor passive** list to 7.0.0; every other tier list on the site is still stamped 6.3.1 (July 2026). That means the weapon and stratagem ratings below are pre-patch community consensus applied to a post-patch game. The factual data (what exists, what changed, unlock sources) is current. The rankings are not.
>
> Items touched by 7.0.0 are flagged with ⚠ in the full tables. Re-check u.gg around the start of September 2026.

Tiers run `S+ > S > A > B > C > D`. **Bots** = Automatons, **Bugs** = Terminids, **Squids** = Illuminate. **AP** = armor penetration class.

## What changed in 7.0.0

The four things most likely to affect what you bring:

> [!success] Assisted reload no longer needs the wielder to carry the backpack
> Either person can hold the ammo pack. In a duo this means one of you runs Recoilless, Spear or Airburst with a **free backpack slot** while the other carries the pack. That trade used to be bad enough that most duos skipped it entirely.

> [!success] Anti-Tank Mines crossed the demo 40 threshold
> Demolition strength went from 30 to 40. They now destroy bug holes and bot fabricators **from outside**, and they now qualify for Bile Titan bug holes, which Thermite at demo 30 still cannot do. Their A rating against bugs is pre-buff.

> [!success] Eagle Napalm Airstrike got a real buff
> Explosion radius 8m to 10m plus a wider bomb pattern. Its B/A/S rating is pre-buff.

> [!warning] Two new Illuminate enemies punish burst-damage builds
> The **Crusher** regenerates while taking heavy damage and must be worn down without pause. The **Wretch** dodges, leaps and tries to disarm you. Both favour sustained DPS. On squid fronts this argues for Stalwart, Bullet Storm, Flamethrower or Cremator over one-shot anti-tank, and the Ultimatum-as-pocket-AT trick is weaker than it was.

Also: level cap raised to 300, SEAF allies now spawn as a planet modifier, heavy landing recovery cut 40%, and a batch of ammo buffs to short magazines and anti-personnel sidearms (Liberator family, Defender, Pummeler, Breaker family, Stoker, Verdict, M6C, Redeemer). The R-2124 Constitution finally has a stripper clip reload.

## Co-op loadout doctrine

This is the section that matters most, and it is where generic tier lists are least useful, because almost all of them assume a four-stack of equally equipped players. The default mode here is a duo with a brand new second player, which is a different problem entirely.

### Even-skill duo

Applies when the second slot is a competent player, so mostly randoms and the occasional trio or quad.

**Split anti-tank and generalist.** One person carries dedicated AT, one runs the Purifier plus Autocannon generalist kit. Do not both bring AT; you will drown in chaff with nothing to clear it. Do not both run generalist either, because two Autocannons is a lot of overlapping coverage and no answer to a Bile Titan or a Factory Strider.

**Post-7.0.0, the AT slot got better.** With the assisted reload rework, the AT carrier can run Recoilless while the generalist carries the ammo pack. The generalist keeps their own backpack free for Supply Pack. This was not practical before.

**Boosters scale with bodies.** Two players, two boosters. Hellpod Space Optimization plus Stamina Enhancement is the default. If either of you runs a stim-heavy build, swap Stamina for Experimental Infusion. Increased Reinforcement Budget takes you from 10 lives to 12 in a duo, which matters more on D9 and D10 than any damage booster. At trio or quad the extra slots go to Vitality Enhancement and Muscle Enhancement before anything situational.

**Sentries are more dangerous with two people than four.** Tesla Towers, Mortar Sentries, Gatling Sentries and Incendiary Mines are the notorious teamkillers. In a duo you are more likely to be in each other's firing lines simply because there are fewer bodies spread across the map.

**Ultimatum covers the AT gap without a stratagem slot.** If neither of you wants to give up a support weapon to anti-tank, the GP-20 Ultimatum in the secondary slot is S+ on bots and bugs. It drops to A on squids, and the new Crusher makes that worse.

### Mixed-skill duo, the default mode

> [!danger] Everything above assumes both players are equipped
> Your brother started in August 2026 and has run roughly ten missions. He has almost no stratagems, no warbonds, and no ship modules, which means long Eagle cooldowns and few uses per call-in. Do not hand him a loadout out of the per-faction lists below; most of it is gear he cannot select.

**Drop the difficulty band.** Every rating in this document assumes Difficulty 7 and above. That band does not apply to his sessions. Below roughly D5 almost everything works and the rankings stop mattering, which is the point: let him use what he has.

**You carry, he learns.** Take the AT and the objective clear yourself, since demo 40 and anti-tank are the two things he cannot cover. Leave him a forgiving kit: high ammo, no charge-up, no self-damage. The free Mobilize gear does this fine, and the Machine Gun or Stalwart with a Supply Pack is the standard new-player answer.

**Cut every friendly-fire footgun.** With 88 friendly kills across 237 missions you already teamkill about once every three missions, and that was against a player who knew to move. Tesla Tower, Mortar Sentry, Gatling Sentry, Incendiary Mines and the 380mm barrage all come out. Orbital Laser and Eagle Strafing Run stay, because they are the two easiest to read from the ground.

**Reinforcements are the real budget.** He will die a lot and reinforcements are shared. Increased Reinforcement Budget is worth more in these sessions than any damage booster, and it costs him nothing to slot since it is free from Mobilize.

**Sample farming is worth more to him than to you.** He has no ship modules at all, so common samples convert directly into a better ship. Liquid-Ventilated Cockpit at 80 common samples halves his Eagle cooldown and is the single largest upgrade available to him.

## Per-faction loadouts

### Automatons (bots)

- **Primary**: Purifier, Eruptor or Crossbow. R-72 Censor or R-63 Diligence if you want a marksman (both S).
- **Secondary**: Ultimatum or Senator, both S+.
- **Support**: Autocannon, Recoilless, C4 Pack or EAT-411 Leveller (all S+).
- **Grenade**: Thermite or Stun.
- **Stratagems**: Eagle Airstrike, Eagle 500kg, Orbital Laser, Orbital Gatling Barrage.
- **Armor**: True Grit, Med-Kit, Concussive Padding Reinforced, Reduced Signature or Fortified (all S+). Medium or Heavy weight, since bot fire is ranged and hard to dodge.

### Terminids (bugs)

- **Primary**: Purifier, Eruptor or Coyote. Blitzer for melee-range chaff shredding. Breaker Incendiary (A) if you want a shotgun.
- **Secondary**: Grenade Pistol or Ultimatum.
- **Support**: Autocannon, Recoilless, Cremator or EAT-17 (all S+).
- **Grenade**: Thermite, Incendiary Impact or Incendiary.
- **Stratagems**: Eagle 500kg, Eagle Airstrike, Orbital Laser, Orbital Airburst Strike, Orbital Gas Strike.
- **Armor**: Med-Kit, True Grit, Desert Stormer, Acclimated or Inflammable (all S+). Light weight unless facing Predator or Rupture strains.

### Illuminate (squids)

- **Primary**: Purifier, Coyote, Punisher Plasma or Torcher.
- **Secondary**: Talon, Loyalist, Grenade Pistol or Crisper.
- **Support**: Autocannon, Cremator, Stalwart, Flamethrower, Airburst, W.A.S.P. or Bullet Storm (all S+).
- **Grenade**: Thermite, Gas or Incendiary Impact.
- **Stratagems**: Eagle Strafing Run, Orbital Laser, Autocannon Sentry, Tesla Tower, Machine Gun Sentry.
- **Armor**: Med-Kit or True Grit. Light weight.

> [!warning] Do not bring these to squid missions
> Recoilless Rifle, Quasar Cannon, EAT-17, Spear, Railgun and Anti-Materiel Rifle all rate C or worse against Illuminate. Eagle 110mm Rocket Pods, Orbital Railcannon and Anti-Tank Mines are D. The 500kg drops to B; use Strafing Run instead.

## Notable movers and traps

> [!failure] The Sickle fell off and has not come back
> LAS-16 Sickle topped every list through 2025. It is now B/B/B. If you are still auto-picking it out of habit, stop. The Double-Edge Sickle (A/A/A) is the better laser primary, at the cost of damaging you while venting.

> [!tip] The two best-value orbitals are not the flashy ones
> Orbital Gas Strike (75s) and Orbital Gatling Barrage (70s) are both S+ on two fronts with cooldowns under 80 seconds. You get several per mission instead of one or two. Orbital 380mm HE Barrage is the trap: 240s cooldown for B/B/C.

> [!tip] Hangar modules are the highest-value ship upgrades in the game
> Liquid-Ventilated Cockpit cuts Eagle cooldown by 50% for 80 common samples. Buy it first.

> [!info] Demolition force thresholds
> **Demo 20** works only when thrown inside the weak spot. **Demo 40** destroys bug holes and fabricators from outside. **Bile Titan bug holes need demo 40.** Your demo 40 options are TED-63 Dynamite, G-48 Giga Grenade and, as of this patch, MD-17 Anti-Tank Mines.

## Warbond priority

For meta value rather than cosmetics:

1. **Democratic Detonation** (Eruptor, Crossbow, Grenade Pistol, Thermite). Four separate S+ items. Nothing else is close.
2. **Polar Patriots** (Purifier, Incendiary Impact). The single best primary in the game.
3. **Castellan's Creed** (True Grit passive on both a medium and a heavy set). Legendary tier, 1,500 Super Credits, no reclaimable Super Credits inside. The passive alone justifies it; the four weapons are unrated.
4. **Servants of Freedom** (Ultimatum, Portable Hellbomb, Double-Edge Sickle).
5. **Dust Devils** (Coyote, Speargun, Solo Silo, Expendable Napalm).
6. **Cutting Edge** (Punisher Plasma, Stun Grenade, Blitzer).

Free from Mobilize and still viable: Scorcher, Diligence, Impact Grenade, Redeemer, Breaker, Hellpod Space Optimization, Stamina Enhancement, Vitality Enhancement, Increased Reinforcement Budget.

> [!warning] Halo: ODST is the weakest Legendary Warbond
> Four weapons, none rated above A on any front, and the Feet First armor passive is B/D/C. At 1,500 Super Credits with nothing reclaimable, it is a cosmetic purchase.

## Known cross-source disagreements

Tier lists are subjective vote aggregates, not measurements. Where credible sources split by a full tier, that is worth knowing rather than averaging away.

- **SG-8 Punisher Plasma**: u.gg rates it S+/S/S+. GamesRadar rates it B/B/B. That is a three-tier gap on the same weapon, which is unusual and suggests it is highly dependent on playstyle or difficulty. Treat the S+ with suspicion until you have tried it yourself.
- **Difficulty assumption**: u.gg does not state a target difficulty. This document assumes Difficulty 7 and above. Below that, almost everything works and the rankings stop mattering.
